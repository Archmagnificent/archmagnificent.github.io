About / Background

Curiosity drives the work.

Video games have always been my biggest passion. I spend a lot of my free time coming up with ideas for games, game mechanics, or world building. While playing games, I often seek solutions to problems that I see in them. Be it balance, level design, or the game's main principles. I'm always looking towards any opportunity to engage my creativity.

Background

Ever since I was a child I was passionate about video games. At some point I started to actively consider the possibility of pursuing a career in game development. From that point I enrolled in college in the subject with classes focusing on game development, worked as a Quality Assurance Tester, completeded my personal project and worked as a gameplay designer in an indie studio. Along the way I have realised that my passion is not limited to the video games as a medium, but also to all types of creative works. As such, I have begun exploring other creative fields, such as writing and art.
