Indoor Farmer

One of my abandoned solo projects.

Project type: Prototype · Role: Game Developer · Status: Abandoned

Overview

The game would revolve around a farm, kept indoors. Due to dangers outside during the nigh, the player would have to gather resources and tend to their plants during the day. The game included changing time of day, and an in-testing, trader NPC. However, changing priorities made me abandon the project.
