Scryer 13

A project, inspired by Iron Lung, focused around interacting with a spaceship via a console.

Project type: Prototype · Role: Game Developer · Status: Postponed

Overview

A project inspired by Iron lung. During the game, the player would take the role of a psychic, using a combination of ship's peripherals and the character's psychic abilities to investigate a space mission with whom contact was lost. The player uses the terminal to interact with the ship's system, plan a location to travel to and then use astral projection to investigate.
