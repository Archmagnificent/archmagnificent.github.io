Scene 01

A Quiet Conversation

The room is dimly lit. Rain taps against the window. Character A stands by the door.

Character A

You said this place would be empty.

Character B

I said it should be empty. That's not the same thing.

A floorboard creaks somewhere in the next room.

Character A

Please tell me that was you.

Character B

I would, if I thought you'd believe me.
