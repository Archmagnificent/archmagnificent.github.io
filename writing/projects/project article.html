Overview

Phantom Resonance

Phantom Resonance (known under a large number of aliases since antiquity) is a genetic trait, characterised by the ability to perceive, and/or interact with Phantom Energy fields. Most commonly manifested, through a presence of modified organs. Phantom resonance is sometimes referred to as "magic" or "sorcery".

While it is technically possible for most sentient lifeforms to develop a small degree of phantom-resonant abilities, the phenomenon most often refers to beings with intrinsic capacity for these abilities.

Individuals possessing Phantom Resonant abilities, are referred to as: phantopaths and phantomorphs.

History / Etymology

Although mentions of supernatural abilities, often referred to as "magic" date back to as early as Neolithic, the ability was not in the scientific context until the 19th century. In 1874, a French physician René Gillard, noted that a number of amputee patients, reported sensations in their phantom limbs when interacting with a specific patient of the hospital. The patient most likely possessed Phantom Resonant abilities without being aware of it himself.

Upon further investigation, the physician discovered, that the patient could interact with the phantom limbs of the amputee patients. He coined the term "Phantom Resonance" based on his initial interpretations of the newly discovered phenomena. He wrote:

[...] the man's presence, not only is capable of arousing sensations, recently labelled as "phantom pains" in the patient's missing limbs. But can also cause reactions in the rest of the limb, to which the phantom was attached, by merely manipulating the area where patients describe the sensation of their etheral limb. It is, as if the man could "tune in" to the etheral matter we cannot perceive.

The discovery of the phenomeNON, was met with skepticism and ridicule from the scientific community at the time, due to the difficulty of experiment replication, and empiric evidence. Despite that, a number of scientists took interest in Gillard's findings. Ideas about supernatural energies within living beings were nothing new. From the folk beliefs from every culture, in things such as qi, ki, mana, or theories of Franz Mesmer. Gillard's findings differed, howver, in that they offered an experimental approach.

Description

Phantom Resonant abilities can generally be divided into two types based on the processes involved in their control and activation:

  • Conscious: Require conscious awareness of the user.
  • Unconscious: Activated automatically as a response of a given organ, or controlled on an instinctual level.

These abilities are not mutually exclusive, as it is possible for an organism to possess both conscious and unconscious phantom resonant abilities.

Conscious

Conscious usage of phantom-resonant abilities is usually reserved only for sentient organisms, possessing a level of self-awareness.

Individuals possessing a natural capacity for phantom-resonance, are known as phantopaths. Although there is a great variation and depth to the types of abilities employed by phantopaths, the basic principle remains the same. Through a specific organ, in case of humans the pineal gland, a phantopath establishes a connection to another dimension. Most typically the Phantom World. The phantopath can then command Phantom Energy directly, or impose the rules of the connected dimension on top of Base Reality.

See more: Phantopath.

Unconscious

Unconscious employment of phantom-resonant abilities, most often includes specialised organs, which upon activation through ordinary biological processes, deploy phantom-resonant abilities.Being possessed of such a capacity are known as phantomorphs.

See more: Phantomorph.
